About Market Maven

Empowering retail traders with verified insights and community-driven strategy sharing.

What is Market Maven?

Market Maven is a platform for retail traders to share, discuss, and learn from real, verifiable trades. Every position shown on the app is authenticated through a secure connection to the user's brokerage account, ensuring that all posts reflect actual market activity — not speculation.

Why it Matters

In a world where misinformation and unproven strategies flood trading communities, Market Maven brings transparency. Posts are only prompted after a real trade is detected, meaning every strategy comes from real money decisions, not hypotheticals.

How It Works

  • Connect your trading account securely via our API integration
  • Get prompted to post when you make a new trade
  • Follow other traders, learn from their strategies, and build your rank
  • Track your performance and reflect on your decision-making over time

What Makes It Different

Unlike typical trading communities, we verify every trade via API integration with brokers like Alpaca. This makes Market Maven the only social platform built on real trades, real data, and real insights.

Who Built It?

Market Maven is a student-led project developed at the University of Miami, created by engineers passionate about fintech, machine learning, and empowering everyday traders through smarter tools.
